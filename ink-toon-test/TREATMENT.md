# Treatment · «Крысы Ханоя» (style: Ink Diorama Toon)

Voice: public/audio/voice.mp3 (74.6 s). Vertical 1080×1920. One idea per shot, cut on the phrase.

## Three candidate structures

1. **Chronicle** — follow the facts in order: street → sewer → office → alley → farm → office → street. Clear, faithful to the narration; risk: flat middle.
2. **Ledger frame** — the official's ledger opens every chapter (an insert), the story plays between pages. Strong motif; risk: inserts steal time from characters.
3. **Two sides of the desk** — every chapter cuts between the official (who pays) and the catcher (who gets paid); the ending puts them face to face over the coin pile. Strong comedy of incentives; risk: the narration names inspectors and farms that need their own shots.

**Choice: 1 + the ending of 3.** The narration is a chronicle, so the shots follow it phrase by phrase; the catcher is set up early (he reads the poster) and the last shot is his triumph on the coin pile: "the metric you pay for".
